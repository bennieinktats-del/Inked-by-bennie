Policies

PLEASE READ BEFORE BOOKING

Please read all Inked by Bennie policies carefully before submitting your booking request. By submitting the form, you confirm that you have read, understood, and agreed to comply with these policies.

Deposit & Payment

A 50% deposit is required to secure your appointment. Your appointment is not confirmed until the required deposit has been successfully received.

Cancellation & Refunds

Cancellations made 7 days or more before the appointment are eligible for a refund of the deposit. Cancellations made less than 7 days before the appointment are non-refundable.

Deposits are also non-refundable in the event of a no-show.

Rescheduling

Rescheduling requests made at least 7 days before the appointment can be made without a rescheduling fee. Requests made less than 7 days before the appointment are subject to a $30 rescheduling fee.

Lateness

A 30-minute grace period is provided for each appointment. Arriving 30–40 minutes late will result in a $20 lateness fee. Arriving more than 40 minutes late will result in cancellation of the appointment and the deposit will be non-refundable.

Age Requirement

Clients must be 18 years or older to receive a tattoo. Valid identification may be requested to verify age.

Guests

A maximum of one guest is permitted during your appointment.

Flash & Custom Designs

Flash designs are intended to be tattooed as displayed. Requests for modifications may be submitted by email and must be approved before the appointment.

Significant changes to a flash design may be considered a custom design and may require a different price.

Skin & Allergies

Clients must disclose any known skin allergies, sensitivities, previous reactions to tattoo products, or other relevant skin concerns before their appointment.

Please do not arrive with sunburned, irritated, infected, or otherwise compromised skin in the area being tattooed.

Photography & Portfolio

Finished tattoo work may be photographed and used by Inked by Bennie for portfolio, website, and social media purposes.

Booking Agreement

By submitting your booking request, you confirm that the information you have provided is accurate and that you agree to follow all Inked by Bennie policies.
